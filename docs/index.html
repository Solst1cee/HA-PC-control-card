PC Control Card

A Lovelace custom card for Home Assistant. Turn on, sleep, shutdown your PC with live CPU / GPU usage & temperatures, RAM, and per‑disk metrics. Three variants, one file, follows your HA theme.

Try it in Home Assistant

Install via HACS as a custom repository, then paste this into a Manual card. The snippet below reflects your live demo choices — accent color, visible buttons, etc.

NAS · Synology (feature)

The same card as a NAS profile: per‑volume usage bars (Volume 1 & 2) plus per‑drive health — a status dot & temperature for Drive 1 & 2 — with uptime and turn on / restart / shut down. Driven entirely by the built‑in Synology DSM integration; no agent on the NAS. The buttons are live too.

NAS config (Synology DSM)

The exact config behind the NAS card above — swap in your own entity IDs. Per‑volume usage and per‑drive health/temperature come straight from the Synology DSM integration; pair with a Wake‑on‑LAN switch for power‑on.

Buttons in the demo above are live — clicking them mutates a mocked Home Assistant state so you can preview every transition. MIT · source
