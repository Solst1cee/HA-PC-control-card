# PC Control Card

A Home Assistant Lovelace card for controlling PCs and NAS devices with three layouts:
`tile`, `chip`, and `feature`.

- Turn on, sleep, restart, and shutdown actions
- CPU, GPU, RAM, temperature, storage, graph, and custom metrics
- Custom values from states or attributes, with units, templates, bars, graphs, and configurable maxima
- Independent logical volumes and physical-drive health monitoring
- Uptime, confirmations, custom icons, accent colors, and HA theme support
- Visual editor, single JavaScript file, and no build step

See [README.md](README.md) for installation and configuration details.
